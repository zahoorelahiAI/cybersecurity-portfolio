# Professional Statement

I am a technical SEO specialist and AI automation builder who is now building a career in cybersecurity. My attention to detail, problem-solving skills and drive to keep learning, along with my commitment to protecting people and acting with integrity, help me find website and system weaknesses before they become threats. I am excited to use these strengths to help organizations protect their data, systems and customers' trust.

---

## My Strengths

- **Attention to detail:** Through technical SEO, I audit websites for small issues like crawl errors, broken links, indexing problems and misconfigured settings that can cause big problems.
- **Problem-solving:** I find the root cause of a technical problem, fix it, and check that the fix worked.
- **Continuous learning and adaptability:** I teach myself new tools and skills quickly. Right now I'm learning AI and building AI workflows and automations.

## My Values

- **Protecting people and organizations:** I want businesses and their customers to be able to trust that their data and systems are safe.
- **Honesty and integrity:** I report what I find accurately, even when it's bad news, and I follow ethical and legal standards.

## Why Cybersecurity

My work in technical SEO taught me how websites are built, crawled and indexed, and showed me how easily a small misconfiguration can expose a site. As I build AI agents and automations, I keep seeing how much sensitive business data runs through these systems. I want to learn how to find and close security gaps before attackers find them.

## How I Can Support an Organization's Security Goals

My attention to detail helps me spot vulnerabilities and unusual activity. My problem-solving skills help me investigate and resolve issues methodically. My commitment to protecting people and acting with honesty means I will handle sensitive information responsibly and report risks clearly, which helps organizations protect their data, their systems and their customers' trust.
