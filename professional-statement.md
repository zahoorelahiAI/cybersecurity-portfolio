## Step 1 – My strengths
*List 2–3 strengths you have now or are working on (e.g., communication, time management, attention to detail, problem-solving).*

1. **Attention to detail** – Through technical SEO, I audit websites for small issues like crawl errors, broken links, indexing problems and misconfigured settings that can cause big problems.
2. **Problem-solving** – I find the root cause of a technical problem, fix it, and check that the fix worked.
3. **Continuous learning and adaptability** – I teach myself new tools and skills quickly. Right now I'm learning AI and building AI workflows and automations.

## Step 2 – My values
*List 1–2 values (e.g., protecting organizations, protecting people, following laws, honesty).*

1. **Protecting people and organizations** – I want businesses and their customers to be able to trust that their data and systems are safe.
2. **Honesty and integrity** – I report what I find accurately, even when it's bad news, and I follow ethical and legal standards.

## Step 3 – Clarifying questions

**What interests me most about cybersecurity?**

My work in technical SEO taught me how websites are built, crawled and indexed, and showed me how easily a small misconfiguration can expose a site. As I build AI agents and automations, I keep seeing how much sensitive business data runs through these systems. I want to learn how to find and close security gaps before attackers find them.

**Who is my professional statement for?**
*(e.g., cybersecurity recruiters, specific companies, government employers)*

Cybersecurity recruiters and hiring managers, especially at digital agencies, tech companies and businesses with an online presence that serve international markets.

**How can my strengths, values, and interest support an organization's security goals?**

My attention to detail helps me spot vulnerabilities and unusual activity. My problem-solving skills help me investigate and resolve issues methodically. My commitment to protecting people and acting with honesty means I will handle sensitive information responsibly and report risks clearly, which helps organizations protect their data, their systems and their customers' trust.
